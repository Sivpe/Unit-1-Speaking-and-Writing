Part 1 · Speaking — At the sports center

50 points

Gatestown Sports Center

Rock climbing classes

  • Sign up for one of our rock climbing classes with an experienced instructor.
  • We offer a one-day introductory class, plus two-day and four-day classes.
  • All classes are good for complete beginners and for those with indoor climbing experience.
  • One-day class: full price $90. Sports center members get 20% off.

The conversation — Kyle and Pam

Kyle: Hi. (1) ______ rock climbing?

Pam: Yes, I mean, I’m thinking of taking a class.

Kyle: (2) ______ members of the sports center get a discount?

Pam: No, I didn’t. But how do I decide which class to choose?

Kyle: If you don’t have any experience, you could try the one-day introductory class. You do some basic climbing and learn something about using ropes.

Pam: And do I need to buy any equipment?

Kyle: No, the price includes all the climbing equipment and the instruction.

Pam: All right. And how much does it cost?

Kyle: The one-day class is $90, but with the 20 percent discount it’s $72.

Pam: OK. Sounds good. So what do I have to do?

Kyle: Choose your dates and fill out a registration form.

Pam: (5) ______! That’s my bus. I have to go. Nice talking to you. (6) ______, what’s the instructor like?

Kyle: You’re looking at him. See you in class!

Part 2 · Writing — History of a sport

50 points

The history of skateboarding — the five paragraphs (a–e)

aAnother famous name is Alan Gelfand who created the “ollie,” the first trick people learn when skateboarding.

bThe first skateboarding competition was held in 1963, but it wasn’t until the 1970s that the sport became popular.

cToday, skateboarding is extremely popular, but it can also be dangerous. At one point, the use and sale of skateboards were banned in Norway!

dMost people say skateboarding started in California, USA, in the 1950s, when surfing was popular. The surfers wanted to find a way to surf on the pavement after they came out of the water.

eThis popularity was largely due to the Z-Boys, a group of surfers from a poor area of Los Angeles. They used the sides of empty swimming pools to skateboard, and the sport became faster and more exciting.
